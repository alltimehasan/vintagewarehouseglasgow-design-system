A brick band of uppercase words with discs between them, like the lettering on a shop awning. It runs under the hero on the homepage, and may run above the footer on other pages; nowhere else.

```html
<ul class="vwg-strip">
  <li>Vintage</li>
  <li>New</li>
  <li>Sold by the bag</li>
  <li>UK delivery only</li>
  <li>Glasgow</li>
</ul>
```

- Five words or short phrases at most, in `on-brick` on `brick`; the discs are the logo's disc, small.
- It is static. No scrolling marquee, in either theme.
- Wraps to two lines on phones; the discs come with the items.
- The strip is the one place brick is used as a full-width fill.
