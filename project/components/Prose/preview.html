Delivery and returns

How your bag gets to you, and what to do if it is not right.

Every bag is packed by hand in the warehouse and sent by tracked courier. We deliver to UK addresses only.

Delivery

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are posted the same day. Delivery is £12.00 per order, however many bags you add.

Returns

You have 14 days from delivery to tell us you want to return a bag, and another 14 days to send it back. Bags are sold as mixed lots, so we cannot swap single pieces.

How to return a bag

  1. Email us with your order number within 14 days of delivery.
  2. Pack the bag as it arrived, with everything in it.
  3. Send it tracked to the address below. We refund within 14 days of getting it back.

What we cannot take back

  • Bags that have been split or partly sold on.
  • Anything washed, altered or damaged after delivery.

Your statutory rights are not affected. If something arrives damaged, tell us and we will put it right.
