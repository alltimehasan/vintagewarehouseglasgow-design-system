Foundations

Layout

A 4px spacing scale, square corners by default, and borders instead of shadows.

Spacing

4px base. Nine steps; nothing in between.

space-1
4px Icon-to-label gap; between a script lead-in and its heading.
space-2
8px Inside badges; between a label and its input; between card lines.
space-3
12px Between buttons in a row; the badge inset on a product photo.
space-4
16px Card padding; the side gutter on phones.
space-6
24px The desktop side gutter; gaps between product cards; button side padding.
space-8
32px Between blocks inside a section; footer column gap.
space-12
48px Section padding on phones; the footer's top padding.
space-16
64px Section padding on desktop.
space-24
96px The hero's top padding; the window pitch on the cover.

Radius

Square by default, like the warehouse. Round only what is a disc or a pill.

radius-none
0 Cards, images, sections, tables, the invoice: everything that is not a control.
radius-sm
2px Buttons and inputs.
radius-full
9999px Badges, the basket count, the logo disc.

Shadow and focus

Borders, not shadows. One exception for things that float.

focus-ring
Keyboard focus on every control: a 2px gap in the page colour, then 2px of solid brick (box-shadow, so it follows the radius). 3:1 or better on every surface in both themes.
shadow-drawer
The basket drawer and the phone menu only.
